What we build

Practical apps for real Botswana work.

We design and set up Power Apps that staff can actually use — not bloated systems that sit unused.

  1. 01 Approvals & requests Leave, purchases, expense and job approvals on phone or desktop.
  2. 02 Inventory & stock checks Track items, counts and simple stock movements without a full ERP.
  3. 03 Job cards & field forms Technicians and staff capture work done, photos and signatures on site.
  4. 04 Excel & Microsoft 365 connected Connect to Excel, SharePoint, Outlook or Teams when that is already how you work.

How TechLab helps

From idea to working app.

You explain the process. We map the screens, build the app, connect the data and train your team.

  1. 01 Process walkthrough We learn who uses the form, what data matters, and where it lives today.
  2. 02 App build & connections Screens, logic, permissions and links to Excel, SharePoint or other Microsoft tools.
  3. 03 Testing & training We test with real users, then show your team how to run and maintain the app.
  4. 04 Support after launch Small changes, fixes and guidance as your process grows.
